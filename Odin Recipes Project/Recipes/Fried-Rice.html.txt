<h1>Fried Rice</h1>

<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8c/Fried_Rice.jpg" width="400">

<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Fried rice is a dish made with cooked rice stir-fried with vegetables, eggs, and meat.</p>

<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul>
<li>Cooked rice</li>
<li>Eggs</li>
<li>Carrots</li>
<li>Green peas</li>
<li>Soy sauce</li>
</ul>

<h2>Steps</h2>
<ol>
<li>Heat oil in a pan.</li>
<li>Add vegetables and eggs.</li>
<li>Add cooked rice.</li>
<li>Season with soy sauce and stir.</li>
</ol>
