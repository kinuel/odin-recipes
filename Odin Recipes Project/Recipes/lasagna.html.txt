<h1>Lasagna</h1>

<img src="https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/lasagna.jpg" alt="Lasagna" width="400">

<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Lasagna is a traditional Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, cheese, and seasonings.</p>
<p>It is baked in the oven until the cheese melts and the flavors combine beautifully.</p>

<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul>
<li>Lasagna noodles</li>
<li>Ground beef</li>
<li>Tomato sauce</li>
<li>Ricotta cheese</li>
<li>Mozzarella cheese</li>
<li>Garlic</li>
<li>Onion</li>
</ul>

<h2>Steps</h2>
<ol>
<li>Cook the noodles.</li>
<li>Prepare the meat sauce.</li>
<li>Layer noodles, sauce, and cheese.</li>
<li>Bake in oven for 40 minutes.</li>
<li>Let it cool and serve.</li>
</ol>
